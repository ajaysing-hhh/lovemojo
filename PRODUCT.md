# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: a lightweight static HTML/CSS/JavaScript MVP for fast iteration before adding a backend and push service.

## Users

Two partners using a private shared space for daily messages, small updates, and lightweight rituals.

## Product Purpose

LoveMojo is a private couple-to-couple messaging app with a curated daily news feed updated by the owner. The first slice should make the shared space feel warm, immediate, and easy to check every day.

## Positioning

A focused two-person space that combines real-time-feeling conversation with a personal daily briefing, instead of mixing a couple's relationship updates into a broad social feed.

## Operating Context

The app is checked on phones throughout the day. One owner publishes the daily feed; both partners read the feed and message each other. Push notifications are a planned next capability.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- MVP: private chat surface, daily feed surface, notification preference control, and responsive mobile-first UI.
- Planned: real peer-to-peer transport, authentication, owner-only feed publishing, push notifications, media sharing, and additional couple features one at a time.
- No backend, credentials, or real push delivery are available in this first slice.

## Brand Commitments

The product name is LoveMojo. The tone should feel intimate, optimistic, personal, and calm rather than like a generic social network.

## Evidence on Hand

No production copy, media, or brand assets supplied. The prototype uses clearly illustrative content and should not be treated as live data.

## Product Principles

- Keep the private relationship at the center.
- Make the daily check-in feel rewarding, not noisy.
- Prefer one clear action over feature overload.
- Add capabilities incrementally without losing emotional warmth.
- Build an invite-only community where every contribution has a clear audience.
- Keep friends as thoughtful spectators: they can suggest, react, and support, but lovers control what enters their private world.
- Connect the loop: suggestion → lover review → shared ritual/feed item → memory.
- Make moderation visible and human-led by the LoveMojo owner.

## Community Model

- Community access is invite-only.
- Lovers own the private couple space and approve friend access.
- Friends participate as spectators and contributors to suggestions, not as equal members of a couple space.
- The LoveMojo owner can approve, hide, or remove community content and revoke access.
- Public MOLO content is the only intentionally public surface.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use readable contrast, visible focus states, semantic controls, and touch targets suitable for mobile use.
