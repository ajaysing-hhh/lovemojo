# LoveMojo visual direction

## Surface

The first surface is an Operate-mode daily home for two partners: a fast check-in, one daily note, and a clear route into their private conversation.

## World

LoveMojo borrows from the intimacy of a personal scrapbook and the calm utility of a handwritten daily ritual. The interface uses warm paper, ink, coral, sage, and sun-yellow rather than the cold gradients of a generic social app.

## Type

- Fraunces carries the intimate, editorial voice of greetings and messages.
- DM Sans keeps navigation and body copy readable.
- DM Mono is reserved for dates, labels, and small status signals.

## Composition

The desktop layout keeps navigation quiet on the left and gives the daily note the strongest visual weight. On mobile, navigation becomes a bottom bar and the daily card preserves its hierarchy without requiring a second screen.

## Interaction language

Actions are small and direct: a round arrow moves between spaces, a single-line composer keeps messaging lightweight, and toasts make prototype actions explicit. Notifications are opt-in and visibly toggleable.
